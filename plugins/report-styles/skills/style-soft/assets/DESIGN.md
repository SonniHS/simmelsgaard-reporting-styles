# Soft · for HR / People

<!-- Generated by report-styles/scripts/build_styles.py from assets/tokens/soft.json; do not edit. -->

## Overview

Warm page, rounded cards, calm colours, generous spacing and larger text; IBCS notation kept in a softer ink. Light: off-white and white cards; dark: warm charcoal.

**Not IBCS-compliant.** It keeps a core of the IBCS® notation (scenarios, variances with impact colours, N/A, same unit same scale) and relaxes the rules listed under Do's and Don'ts. Where IBCS compliance is required, use the IBCS style.

Every style draws the same report page: a key message, four KPI tiles, a time card and a structure card, in the same order and the same columns. Only the look changes; the numbers, their positions and the notation rules do not.

## Colors

Tokens without a prefix are the light theme (the default for print and documents); `dark-*` is the dark theme. Text reaches WCAG AA on every surface it sits on, checked by the build.

- **Primary (#7A68B8):** the accent for the selected switch and focus; never a data colour.
- **Neutral (#FBF7F2) and Surface (#FFFFFF):** the page and the cards.
- **Scenario AC (#4A4E69) and PY (#C9C5D6):** actuals solid and strong, previous year lighter. PL is outlined and FC hatched in the same ink; scenarios are told apart by pattern, not hue.
- **Impact good (#389A62), bad (#C0392B), neutral (#3D6FC4):** the tone of a variance only. `impact-good-cvd` (#1F8C9E) replaces green in the colour-vision mode.
- **Dark:** page #1C1A21, AC #E4E0EE, PY #6B6578.

## Typography

Text in Nunito Sans, headings in Nunito, figures in Nunito Sans with tabular numerals, so columns of numbers align. The key message is 20 px; KPI values 30 px; chart labels 13 px.

## Layout

4 KPI tiles per row, then the time card and the structure card side by side from 1024 px and stacked below it (time first). Gap 24 px, card padding 28 px, at most 4 visuals on a page. No horizontal scroll at any width.

## Elevation & Depth

Cards and tiles lift off the page with one soft shadow in the light theme; the dark theme uses a border instead. There is no further elevation: charts and tables stay flat.

## Shapes

Corner radii: key message 24 px, cards 20 px, tiles 18 px, controls 12 px, chart marks 4 px.

## Components

- **kpi-tile:** label, AC value, PY, ΔPY and ΔPY%. The value shrinks to fit its tile and is never cut.
- **card:** a title, a subtitle naming unit and scenarios, then the chart.
- **mark-ac / mark-py:** columns and bars; all marks of one unit share one scale across the page.
- **variance-*:** a ΔPY or ΔPY% figure in its impact tone.
- **switch / switch-selected:** style and theme switches; the selection is shown by fill, not weight.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do write a relative variance against the signed reference; show N/A when the reference is zero, negative or rounds to less than half a unit.
- Do let polarity choose the tone only: a variance shown as zero has no colour and no sign.
- Do draw the same unit at the same scale everywhere on the page.
- Do keep every number on the page equal to the view model; every style shows the same set.
- Don't use these chart types: pie, ring/donut, gauge, funnel, radar, traffic light, 3-D, pictogram.
- Don't use the accent or a second hue to tell series apart.

Where this style departs from the IBCS rules:

- **SI 1.2:** Warm off-white page behind white cards (dark: warm charcoal with slightly lighter cards). Calm, familiar look for non-finance readers; charts sit on white.
- **SI 2.2:** AC in a soft blue-grey ink (#4A4E69 light, #E4E0EE dark) instead of a neutral dark grey. The calmer ink is the style; the tint is low-saturation, far from every impact hue, and AC stays clearly stronger than PY (checked). Condition: Never a second colour for a second series; further scenarios keep their IBCS fills.
- **SI 2.1:** Rounded cards (18–24 px) with one soft shadow; bar ends rounded 4 px. Chrome softness; a 4 px radius on a bar of 20+ px keeps the length readable. Condition: No radius on bars thinner than 8 px.
- **CO 1.1:** Larger text (body 15, labels 13 px). Readers outside finance, often on phones.
- **CO 2.2:** Generous spacing; at most 4 visuals a page. Lower density is the point of the style; split a dense page into two. Lower density never means filler: every element carries a message.
- **SI 3.1:** Faint horizontal gridlines where data labels are dropped for readability. Helps readers who do not read value labels first. Condition: Gridlines or data labels on a visual, never both.
- **SI 1.3:** A short fade on entry. Calm feel. Condition: Off under prefers-reduced-motion; numbers are final from the first frame.
