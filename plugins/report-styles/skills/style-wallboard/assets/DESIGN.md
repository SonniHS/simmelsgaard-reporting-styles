# Wallboard · for Operations

<!-- Generated by report-styles/scripts/build_styles.py from assets/tokens/wallboard.json; do not edit. -->

## Overview

A screen read from a few metres: very large figures, high contrast, thick columns and bars, variances as large coloured chips, few labels.

**Not IBCS-compliant.** It keeps a core of the IBCS® notation (scenarios, variances with impact colours, N/A, same unit same scale) and relaxes the rules listed under Do's and Don'ts. Where IBCS compliance is required, use the IBCS style.

Every style draws the same report page: a key message, four KPI tiles, a time card and a structure card, in the same order and the same columns. Only the look changes; the numbers, their positions and the notation rules do not.

## Colors

Tokens without a prefix are the light theme (the default for print and documents); `dark-*` is the dark theme. Text reaches WCAG AA on every surface it sits on, checked by the build.

- **Primary (#0B0F14):** the accent for the selected switch and focus; never a data colour.
- **Neutral (#E9EDF1) and Surface (#FFFFFF):** the page and the cards.
- **Scenario AC (#0B0F14) and PY (#A3ADB9):** actuals solid and strong, previous year lighter. PL is outlined and FC hatched in the same ink; scenarios are told apart by pattern, not hue.
- **Impact good (#1B8A4C), bad (#CC2F27), neutral (#1F63CC):** the tone of a variance only. `impact-good-cvd` (#0A7C90) replaces green in the colour-vision mode.
- **Dark:** page #06090D, AC #F4F7FA, PY #5A6573.

## Typography

Text in Inter, headings in Inter, figures in Inter with tabular numerals, so columns of numbers align. The key message is 24 px; KPI values 56 px; chart labels 15 px.

## Layout

4 KPI tiles per row, then the time card and the structure card side by side from 1024 px and stacked below it (time first). Gap 16 px, card padding 20 px, at most 3 visuals on a page. No horizontal scroll at any width.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Hierarchy comes from the page and card tones and from type weight, not from shadows.

## Shapes

Corner radii: key message 6 px, cards 6 px, tiles 6 px, controls 6 px, chart marks 2 px.

## Components

- **kpi-tile:** label, AC value, PY, ΔPY and ΔPY%. The value shrinks to fit its tile and is never cut.
- **card:** a title, a subtitle naming unit and scenarios, then the chart.
- **mark-ac / mark-py:** columns and bars; all marks of one unit share one scale across the page.
- **variance-*:** a ΔPY or ΔPY% figure in its impact tone.
- **switch / switch-selected:** style and theme switches; the selection is shown by fill, not weight.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do write a relative variance against the signed reference; show N/A when the reference is zero, negative or rounds to less than half a unit.
- Do let polarity choose the tone only: a variance shown as zero has no colour and no sign.
- Do draw the same unit at the same scale everywhere on the page.
- Do keep every number on the page equal to the view model; every style shows the same set.
- Don't use these chart types: pie, ring/donut, gauge, funnel, radar, traffic light, 3-D, waffle, pictogram, area-scaled-icons, truncated-axis.
- Don't use the accent or a second hue to tell series apart.

Where this style departs from the IBCS rules:

- **SI 1.2:** A contrasting page behind the tiles and cards (light: grey; dark: near-black with slate panels). Zones must separate at a glance from a distance; dark keeps a lit-room screen from glaring. Condition: Impact and scenario colours are tuned per mode and contrast-checked; print is always light.
- **CO 1.1:** Very large type: KPI values 56 px, labels 15 px, the message 24 px. Read from a few metres on a wall screen or a team TV.
- **SI 2.1:** Tiles and cards with a 6 px radius on a contrasting page. Separates the zones at a glance; chrome only, the marks carry no shade.
- **CO 2.2:** Fewer labels: AC values on the columns and the ΔPY% row, no PY value labels. A glance, not an analysis; PY stays visible as the reference column and in the KPI tiles. Condition: Every value of the shared set is still printed somewhere on the page.
- **CO 1.3:** At most 3 visuals a page. A wall screen carries one situation; detail lives in the IBCS page.
